Privacy Policy — Loot Timer

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Data Controller

Loot Timer is developed by Maks Rajter, Poland. Contact: rajtermaks1@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

None. Loot Timer has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Everything the app knows — your sessions, streak, coins, items and rooms — is stored only on your iPhone.

Data we do NOT collect: name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, advertising data.

3. App Blocking (Screen Time)

To block distracting apps during a focus session, Loot Timer uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity). You choose the apps in Apple's own picker. Apple gives Loot Timer only opaque tokens for that choice — the app cannot see which apps you picked, which apps you use or for how long. The tokens never leave your device.

4. Notifications, Widgets and Live Activities

Local notifications tell you when a crate is ready. The home-screen widget and the Live Activity show your room, streak and timer. All of this is generated on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

5. In-App Purchases

Loot Timer Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether your subscription is active.

6. Third-Party Services

Apple (App Store, StoreKit, Screen Time). No other third-party services or SDKs are used.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete the app.

8. Children

Loot Timer does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

9. GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

Because no personal data is collected or processed by us, there is nothing to access, export or erase on our side. You can still contact us with any question.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
