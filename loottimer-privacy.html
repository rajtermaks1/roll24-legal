Privacy Policy — Loot Timer

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Data Controller

Loot Timer is developed by Maks Rajter, Poland. Contact: rajtermaks1@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Almost nothing. Loot Timer has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads. Everything the app knows — your sessions, streak, coins, items and rooms — is stored only on your phone.

The only data that leaves your device is your purchase history for Loot Timer Pro, which is handled by RevenueCat (see sections 5 and 6), tied to a random app user ID — not to your name, email or Apple ID.

Data we do NOT collect: name, email, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, advertising data. Nothing is used for tracking.

3. App Blocking (Screen Time)

To block distracting apps during a focus session, Loot Timer uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity). You choose the apps in Apple's own picker. Apple gives Loot Timer only opaque tokens for that choice — the app cannot see which apps you picked, which apps you use or for how long. The tokens never leave your device.

4. Notifications, Widgets and Live Activities

Local notifications tell you when a crate is ready. The home-screen widget and the Live Activity show your room, streak and timer. All of this is generated on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

5. In-App Purchases

Loot Timer Pro is sold through Apple's App Store (and Google Play on Android). The store processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. To check whether Pro is active and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipts (product, price, purchase and renewal dates, country) and a random app user ID generated on your device. It does not receive your name, email or Apple ID. We use this only to unlock Pro and to see aggregate sales numbers.

6. Third-Party Services

Apple (App Store, StoreKit, Screen Time); Google Play Billing on Android; RevenueCat, Inc. (purchase validation, revenuecat.com/privacy). No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are used.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your app data stays on your device until you delete the app. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide Pro and to meet accounting obligations; write to us to have them deleted.

8. Children

Loot Timer does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

9. GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

The only personal data processed is your purchase history (legal basis: performance of the contract for Loot Timer Pro). You can ask us to access, export or erase it — email us with the date of your purchase (the Apple or Google receipt email is enough).

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
